A Lotus Elise.

Lotus launched the Elise in September 1996, named after Elisa Artioli, the
granddaughter of Romano Artioli, then the chairman of Lotus. Its fibreglass
body sits on a bonded chassis of extruded aluminium, and the first cars
weighed 725 kg. That is from
[Wikipedia's article on the Elise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotus_Elise).
