title: Condition register
summary: Every fault found so far, what shows it, what is to be done about it, and where that has got to.
---

Nothing has been moved or measured yet. Everything here so far comes from
Andrew's photographs and from what he has told me, and says so. The register
proper begins with the survey at rest.

| What | Found | How it is known | Decision | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oil-hole plugs, both headstocks | There is a plain hole above each spindle bearing, four in all. The catalogue cutaway shows each closed by a small knurled plug. All four plugs are gone; one hole, at the back of the fitted headstock, has a nipple in it instead. | Andrew, who looked at all four, and the cutaway he found. | Cover the open holes with tape now, to keep grit out of the bearings. Make four plugs once the holes have been measured. Decide about the nipple when it is known whether the cover shuts over it. | Open |
| Ring nuts behind the nose, both headstocks | Bruised beside their notches, as if driven round with a punch. | Photographs of both noses. | Nothing yet. Make a C-spanner to fit before either is next moved. | Noted |
| Collet-closer lever, spare headstock | Missing. Only the stub it fits to is there. | Photograph of the spare. | Nothing yet. It is the spare. | Noted |
| Belt | There is none. | Andrew. | A flat belt has to be found or made before it can run. The catalogue gives one inch wide; to be checked against the pulleys. | Open |
| Motor | Out of the stand. A single-speed Brook, 2 h.p., 400/440 V three-phase, where the lathe's change-pole switch and speed plate are for a motor with two speeds. | Its plate, in a photograph, and the plates on the switches. | Settle at the electrical stage how it is to be supplied and whether the two switches can be kept in use. Find out first whether it has its stepped pulley and was ever fitted to this stand. | Open |
| Pulley cover | Off the headstock and lying in the tray. Andrew has the covers for both headstocks. | Andrew, and the photograph of the whole machine. | Refit after the headstock has been surveyed. | Noted |
| Paint | A grey-blue, flaking, over a turquoise blue that shows at the chips, round the plates and across most of one stand door. Repainted at least once. | Photographs of the plates and the headstock. | Strip and repaint at the finishing stage. Before that, lift a plate to find the colour underneath it. | Noted |
| Bright steel on the closer | The drum and the lever are peppered with rust. | Photograph of the left-hand end. | Finishing stage. | Noted |
