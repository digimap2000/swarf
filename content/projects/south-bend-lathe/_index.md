A South Bend lathe, one more for the collection.

South Bend Lathe Works was founded in 1906 in South Bend, Indiana, by the twin
brothers John and Miles O'Brien. By 1930 it was building nearly half of the
engine lathes sold in the United States each year, and its machines were
fixtures of American machine shops, factories and school workshops from the
1920s to the 1960s, according to
[Wikipedia's history of the company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Bend_Lathe).
Which model this one is, and when it was made, is not yet recorded.
