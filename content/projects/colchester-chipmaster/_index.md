A Colchester Chipmaster, and another lathe for the collection.

The Chipmaster was announced in 1956, and the first left the works in 1957;
the last went out in 1983. It is a 5¾ in centre-height lathe with a full
screwcutting gearbox, and its spindle speed is infinitely variable up to
3,000 r.p.m. through a Kopp variator, a part with a reputation for being
costly to overhaul. All of that is from
[Tony Griffiths' history of the Chipmaster](https://www.lathes.co.uk/chipmaster)
on lathes.co.uk. When this one was built, and what state its variator is in,
is not yet recorded.
